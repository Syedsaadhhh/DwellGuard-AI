# DwellGuard

## A late truck should not derail the whole dock plan.

DwellGuard turns a disrupted delivery into one clear, agreed next step.

It reaches the right people, understands the driver's real arrival window, coordinates the revised appointment, and brings the final plan back to everyone who needs it.

### What makes it different

**One conversation changes the next.**

The driver's latest timing directly shapes what happens with the receiving dock. DwellGuard works inside the limits approved by the dispatcher, keeps the details consistent, and makes uncertainty visible when a decision still needs a person.

### The result

**Late arrival → workable window → agreed appointment → driver informed**

Less chasing. Less confusion. A plan everyone can act on.
